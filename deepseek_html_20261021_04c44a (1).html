How to Play

Move — Drag the round joystick (bottom-left) or tap the ground to walk there.

Interact — When a glowing ✦ is near, tap the big round ✦ button (bottom-right) or the ✦ on screen.

Dialogue — Tap the dark text box to continue. Tap a choice to pick it. Choices shape your Bond and ending.

Menu — 📖 Journal, 🎒 Satchel, 🗺️ Map, ☰ Pause at the top-right.

Save — Pause → Save. Continue from the title screen.
